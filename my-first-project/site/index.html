How it works

  1. 1

    Snap a photo

    Walk your aisles like you always do and take a photo of each shelf with your phone.

  2. 2

    AI counts your shelf

    ShelfSense reads the photo, counts the products, and spots what's running low.

  3. 3

    Send the order

    You get a reorder list for each supplier — checked, sorted, and ready to send.
