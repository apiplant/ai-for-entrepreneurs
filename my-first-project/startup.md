# ShelfSense

## Problem
Anyone who's run a small grocery knows the drill: counting shelves on paper, or just keeping it all in your head. It eats 3–4 hours a week — and you still run out of your best sellers, or order too much fresh stuff that ends up in the trash.

## Customer
We're building for independent grocery stores and minimarkets with 1–3 locations. We went and talked to 14 owners in Milan, and 9 of them said they'd pay for this.

## Solution
Here's the whole thing: you walk the aisles, take a photo of the shelves with your phone, and the app tells you what's running low and builds the reorder list for each supplier. One photo, done.

## Why now
This just wasn't possible a couple of years ago — but last year, phone cameras plus vision AI got good and cheap enough to actually count products from a photo. So the idea finally works.

## Business model
$29/month per store, with a free 14-day trial so owners can see it work on their own shelves before paying.
