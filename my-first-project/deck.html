01ShelfSense

02The problem

Counting shelves eats 3–4 hours a week.

On paper, or from memory. That's how small groceries do inventory today.

03The payoff

3 hours saved
per week.

That's the time every pilot shop gets back from shelf counting, every week.

04The customer

Independent grocery stores and minimarkets with 1–3 locations.

05Validation

9 of 14 owners in Milan said they'd pay for this.

06The solution

Walk the aisles, take a photo — the app builds the reorder list for each supplier.

Photo in, reorder list out. No spreadsheets, no paper.

07Why now

Phone cameras + vision AI finally got good and cheap enough to count products from a photo.

This just wasn't possible a couple of years ago.

08Traction

Live in 3 pilot shops since September.

Each saves about 3 hours a week.

09Business model

$29 per store, per month.

Free 14-day trial, so owners see it work on their own shelves before paying.

10The team

5 years in supermarket supply chain, plus the engineer building it.
