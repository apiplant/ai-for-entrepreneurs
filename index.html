02 · Is it a bubble?

Probably
a bubble.

Doesn't matter.

Yes, I think there's a bubble. No, it doesn't change what you should do.

$$$
Why I think it's a bubble

The big jumps
are over.

Raw intelligence of top models has more or less peaked.

So labs specialise — and smaller, cheaper models beat yesterday's frontier.

Transformers are good enough to automate a big chunk of work. Replacing human judgement needs new research, not just more hardware.
Meanwhile, look at the money

The gold rush is eating the hardware.

Consumer RAM
~0×

64GB kit: ~€170 in mid-2025 → €926 today. Data centres are buying all the memory.

Why it doesn't matter

Even if it bursts,
AI stays.

Worst case: OpenAI and Anthropic disappear tomorrow morning.

Chinese labs have released open models, close to the frontier and good enough for most uses.

Open = download it, modify it, host it anywhere. You can't un-invent that.

Not hype. Just logic: adapt, or be left behind.

For everyone.

Bad news #1

Pressure for even smaller teams.

Everyone can build →
too much of everything.

The most important warning of the day

Trust,
but verify.

AI is not infallible. It hallucinates: stats, quotes, court cases, papers, links.

With total confidence. It doesn't know it's wrong.

market-research.md
made up
"According to a 2024 Harvard study, 87% of founders who use AI reach profitability in year one."
My rules

AI does the first draft. You're the editor.

1

If it cites a source, open the source. Check it exists and says what the AI claims.

2

Check every number that matters.

3

If it writes code, run it and test it. Don't assume it works.

4

Read everything before it goes out with your name on it. If it's wrong, it's on you.

Trick: ask a different AI to find the mistakes.
Still be good at your job — AI just made you 10× faster.
